# Scene library

Pick by product category. Each scene is one continuous shot unless noted.

## Bags, luggage, footwear
- **Slow orbit on stone** — 30 degree arc around the product on a matte stone
  slab, seamless backdrop, soft key from high 45, hard kicker on one edge.
- **Held walk** — product carried at hip height, camera tracks alongside at
  walking pace, shallow depth, street bokeh. Use only when the user asks for
  a person; a model in frame changes the shot from product to lifestyle.
- **Drop-in reveal** — product already resting, camera pushes in from wide to
  a tight three-quarter on the hardware.

## Cosmetics, fragrance, skincare
- **Liquid light** — bottle centred on glass, caustic light patterns drifting
  across the label, slow push-in.
- **Texture beat** — macro on the product's texture (cream swirl, powder
  press), then pull back to the closed package.

## Electronics, wearables
- **Screen-on reveal** — device dark, display wakes as the camera settles,
  rack focus from bezel to screen.
- **Exploded hold** — product floating slightly above a plinth, slow rotation,
  cool key with a single warm rim.

## Packaged goods, food, drink
- **Condensation pour** — chilled pack with beading condensation, slow tilt
  up from base to logo.
- **Shelf hero** — product on a clean shelf, competitors implied out of focus
  behind, gentle dolly in.

## Jewellery, watches
- **Rack focus glint** — locked frame, focus pulls from a foreground edge to
  the stone or dial, single specular travels across as focus lands.

## Timing
- 5-6s: one shot, one move.
- 10s: two shots — wide establish, then macro detail.
- 15s: three shots — establish, detail, hero close. Keep wardrobe, lighting
  and product wording identical across all three prompts.
