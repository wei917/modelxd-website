# Prompt scaffolds

## Clean plate (image_edit, only when the source photo is a poor reference)

Isolate the exact product from the reference image and place it on a pure
seamless white background. Soft even diffused studio lighting, one subtle
contact shadow beneath the product, product centred and fully in frame. No
props, no hands, no people, no text, no watermark, no background objects.
Preserve the product exactly: identical shape, proportions, colour, material,
texture, stitching, hardware and logo placement. Do not redesign or restyle.

## Video (reference-based)

Structure the paragraph in this order and keep it to one paragraph:

1. Name the reference explicitly — "the exact <product> from the reference
   image", never a generic noun.
2. Restate the invariants in a few words — colour, material, hardware finish,
   branding position.
3. Place it in the FIRST frame — where it sits, at what height, at what angle.
4. The camera move — one move, direction, degrees, speed.
5. Light — key direction and quality, one specular behaviour, falloff.
6. Setting — surface and backdrop, and nothing else.
7. Continuity — state that the product stays centred and in frame throughout.

## Never

Do not write a colour, material or branding that contradicts the reference.
Do not let the product enter partway through the shot. Do not add text
overlays, sparkles, lens flares or confetti. Do not combine two camera moves.
